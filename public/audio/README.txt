Optional Background Music Placeholder
==================================

Place your royalty-free or personal instrumental Nikkah background audio file here as:

  public/audio/wedding-music.mp3

Recommendations:
- Soft acoustic, oud, santoor, piano, or nasheed instrumental (MP3, 128kbps, under 3MB for fast mobile loading).
- Keep the audio gentle and loop-friendly.

Note:
- If `wedding-music.mp3` is not present, the website continues to work smoothly without errors
  and offers a soft, optional ambient harmonic chime fallback when the visitor enables sound.
